# Brag Plan: Ecosopis - Cosméticos Naturais

## What is this app?
A modern, direct-to-consumer (DTC) clean beauty e-commerce delivering 100% natural, vegan, and cruelty-free handcrafted skincare, artisanal treatment soaps (like saffron/turmeric), and pure botanical oils across Brazil.

## The angle
"Ditch the harsh chemicals. Your skin deserves pure botanical science."
A vibrant, high-energy modern DTC brand launch that turns natural skincare from a niche hobby into an irresistible daily luxury. We contrast industrial synthetic formulas with handcrafted, earth-grown botanical potency, showcasing gorgeous textures, transparent formulas, and seamless one-click buying.

---

## The 9-Question Planning Rubric

1. **What is the app?**  
   An artisanal clean-beauty e-commerce platform offering 100% natural, vegan, cruelty-free soaps, hair tonics, and cold-pressed botanical oils with nationwide Brazilian delivery.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**  
   "Sua pele absorve 60% do que você passa nela. Você comeria os químicos do seu sabonete convencional?" / "Fórmulas 100% puras com açafrão da terra e óleos botânicos que realmente transformam a pele."

3. **What is the visual hook?**  
   A lush, radiant botanical green (`#4B8411`) explosion on pure milk-white backgrounds, featuring hyper-detailed product cards, golden saffron textures, and smooth mobile-first interface cards.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**  
   The clean product catalog grid with glowing reviews, the artisanal saffron soap detail page, the intuitive cart drawer, and the quick Brazilian checkout badge.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**  
   20 seconds (Vertical 1080x1920 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts). Snappy, rhythmic, and impossible to scroll past.

6. **What tone fits best?**  
   - **Preset:** `polished` / `app-store` hybrid.  
   - **Creative direction:** "Fresh, modern DTC brand launch."  
   - **Interpretation:** Warm, empowering, premium yet accessible, rhythmic motion with crisp typography, natural ASMR-like sound cues, and no corporate fluff.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**  
   Upbeat, organic lo-fi / indie pop acoustic-electronic groove with crisp, satisfying tactile SFX (soft whooshes, botanical water droplets, subtle box clicks, and clean UI confirmation chimes).

8. **What should the share caption say?**  
   "Sua rotina de skincare nunca mais será a mesma: cosméticos 100% naturais, veganos e artesanais entregues na sua porta. Conheça a Ecosopis! 🌱✨"

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**  
   Discovering the artisanal soaps & oils on the feed ➔ Tapping the Saffron Soap with instant benefit tags ➔ Adding to cart with fast shipping calculator ➔ Clean 1-click checkout confirmation.

## Audio Direction
- **Role:** Warm, vibrant, uplifting modern DTC groove (acoustic guitar pluck meets modern snap beat).
- **Tempo:** ~116 BPM, high positivity and momentum.
- **SFX Posture:** Motion-matched tactile cues:
  - 0.0s: Soft organic leaf chime / air whoosh on hook.
  - 3.5s: Crisp card flip / pop on catalog showcase.
  - 7.0s: Droplet / splash sound on saffron soap highlight.
  - 11.5s: Quick digital badge sound on "Cruelty-Free & Vegano".
  - 16.0s: High-satisfaction tap chime on CTA "ecosopis.com.br".
- **Restraint Rule:** Never overpower the message; voiceover / captions lead readability.

## Storyboard (20 Seconds Total)

### Scene 1 — The Botanical Hook (0.0s – 3.5s | Duration: 3.5s)
- **Visual:** Fast, dynamic entrance. Big bold header against fresh organic green gradient background:
  - *"Sua pele merece mais que químicos industriais."*
  - Subtitle: *"O poder do autocuidado 100% natural & sustentável."*
  - Three micro-badges slide in: `🌿 100% Natural` | `🐰 Cruelty-Free` | `✨ Vegano`
- **Sequential:** Badges pop in one-by-one (0.8s hold).
- **Audio intent:** Clean air whoosh into rhythmic beat drop.
- **Transition:** Quick upward mask swipe → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — O Catálogo dos Sonhos (3.5s – 8.0s | Duration: 4.5s)
- **Visual:** Phone frame showing the Ecosopis live catalog. Three hero product cards slide horizontally with smooth parallax:
  1. **Sabonete de Açafrão & Argila:** Clareador natural, combate foliculite e manchas.
  2. **Óleo Vegetal Puro de Rosa Mosqueta:** Regeneração e viço profundo.
  3. **Tônico Capilar Botânico:** Fortalecimento da raiz às pontas.
- **Simulated Interaction:** Finger taps on the Saffron Soap card; a glowing 5-star review pops up: *"Minha pele nunca esteve tão uniforme!"*
- **Audio intent:** Snappy tactile pops on card entrances, subtle liquid droplet on product tap.
- **Transition:** Smooth slide right → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Proposta de Valor & Confiança (8.0s – 14.5s | Duration: 6.5s)
- **Visual:** Split screen / floating benefit cards with clean DTC badges:
  - 📦 **Entrega Rápida para Todo o Brasil** (rastreamento em tempo real)
  - 🧪 **Zero Parabenos, Petrolatos e Corantes Artificiais**
  - 💳 **Compra Segura & Pagamento Facilitado** (Pix, Cartão em até 12x)
  - 💬 **Atendimento Humanizado via WhatsApp Integrado**
- **Sequential:** Cards lock into place sequentially with crisp audit checkmarks (0.9s per card hold).
- **Audio intent:** Cheerful percussion rhythm with soft positive chimes on checkmarks.
- **Transition:** Elegant zoom out into brand finale → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The Grand CTA / Launch Offer (14.5s – 20.0s | Duration: 5.5s)
- **Visual:** The iconic **ECOSOPIS** logo in gold and forest green (`Cinzel` font).
  - Main Headline: *"Renove sua pele com o que a terra tem de melhor."*
  - Big interactive-style button pulsating softly:
    **[ QUERO MINHA PELE RADIANTE ➔ ]**
  - Direct URL display: **`www.ecosopis.com.br`**
  - Delivery ribbon: *"Entrega garantida para todo o Brasil"*
- **Audio intent:** Music builds to a celebratory melodic finish, ending on a crisp bell resonance.
- **Outro Hold:** Holds on the URL and logo for the final 2 seconds for clear viewer retention.
